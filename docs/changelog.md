# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- 60 studio kits in 7 categories, picked from a thumbnail grid.
- Projection kits with real shadow masks, for EEVEE and Cycles.
- Rig controller: move, rotate and scale the whole studio; Fit Rig to
  Selection; Master and Exposure.
- 9 parametric backdrop shapes and a shadow-catcher floor.
- Inline light editor with quick presets.
- Cameras with focal presets and depth of field.
- HDRI browser for your own folder.
- Gobo images and 32 bundled IES profiles (Cycles).
